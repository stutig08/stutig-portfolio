# Data visualization examples

## Visualizing government debt (Tableau)

Two views of general government debt as a share of GDP across OECD countries, 2007 to 2023.

**[Government debt as a share of GDP, OECD countries, 2007 to 2023](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/stuti.garg4065/viz/OECDGovernmentDebttoGDP2007to2023/GovernmentDebtasaShareofGDPOECDCountries2007to2023)**
A highlight table (heatmap) showing each country's debt-to-GDP ratio by year, so readers can scan for countries and years where debt rose sharply.

**[The top never moved; everyone below did](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/stuti.garg4065/viz/OECDGovernmentDebtRank2007to2023/TheTopNeverMoved_EveryoneBelowDid_)**
A bump chart redesign that ranks 35 OECD countries by debt-to-GDP from 2007 to 2023. Spain and Israel are highlighted against grey to show how much a country's rank can change while the countries at the top stay put.

**Data source:** OECD, General government debt (indicator), as provided for the course assignment.
