Your current headings are full questions ("What are these buildings?", "Why does the list keep growing?"). They're too long for the navigation bar and get cut off. Use short one- or two-word **Headings** for the navigation bar. Then put the full question right under each one as a **Subheading**, which doesn't show in the navigation bar.

**How to make them:**
1. Click into the heading line → toolbar **Paragraph** dropdown → **Heading**. Type the short heading.
2. Press Enter for a new line → **Paragraph** dropdown → **Subheading**. Type the subheading.
3. The navigation bar updates automatically. Only Headings appear in it.

### 6. Carbon Cost
**Heading:** Carbon Cost
**Subheading:** What a teardown throws away

Keep your first paragraph. **Delete the "Estimate in progress" paragraph** and replace it with:

> The 640 buildings in the "save first" group (see Triage below) add up to about **1.06 million square feet** of living space. A Carbon Leadership Forum study found that new low-rise housing typically emits **up to 500 kg of CO2e per square meter** just for its structure, foundation, and walls. Rebuilding that much space new could release as much as **49,000 tonnes of CO2e**, before counting interiors, heating systems, or site work.
>
> Renovation isn't carbon-free. But research by the National Trust for Historic Preservation found that reuse almost always beats demolition and new construction, and that a new energy-efficient building can take **10 to 80 years** to make up for the carbon spent building it.
